DFO Gang — Dungeon Fighter Online Character Search & Rankings

DFO Gang is a community-driven platform for Dungeon Fighter Online (DFO) Global players. Search any character across Cain and Sirocco servers, view detailed equipment breakdowns, calculate DPS scores and buff power, track fame history, and compare class rankings — all powered by real-time data from the official Neople Open API.

Character Search & DPS Calculator

Look up any DFO character by name and instantly see their fame, class, equipment set, and DFO Gang Score — our equipment-based damage rating system. The score accounts for all gear stats including set bonuses, enchants, amplification, reinforce levels, creature effects, and buff enhancements. Support classes like Enchantress, Crusader, and Saint receive a dedicated Buff Power score that measures party contribution.

Class Rankings

Compare characters within every advanced class in DFO. Rankings cover all dealer and support classes across both servers, sortable by fame or DFO Gang Score. Track where you stand among fellow players and discover top-geared characters in your class.

Equipment Analysis & History

Inspect full gear breakdowns for any character — weapons, armor, accessories, special equipment, and earrings with per-slot detail on enchants, fusion stones, amplification, and reinforce. Fame and gear history tracking lets you see how a character has progressed over time.

Class Statistics

Explore aggregated data across the DFO playerbase. See which equipment sets, fusion stones, VP selections, and buff enhancements the top performers in each class are using. Statistics are calculated from characters above the fame threshold, giving you a real picture of endgame trends.

Community

Join the DFO Gang community forum. Create posts, leave comments, vote on content, tag topics, use custom stickers, and discuss strategies with other DFO players. Whether you are looking for build advice, sharing your latest gear, or just chatting — the community tab has you covered.
