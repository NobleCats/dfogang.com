Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 14, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Username — Used as your public display name.
  • Email address (Gmail only) — Used for account verification and recovery.
  • Password — Stored securely as a one-way hash; we never store or see your plain-text password.
  • Profile picture (optional) — Compressed and stored on our servers.

When you use the site, we may automatically collect:

  • IP address — Used for rate limiting, abuse prevention, and view counting.
  • Browser user-agent — Included in standard HTTP requests.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the service (account authentication, post/comment features).
  • To send verification emails during registration.
  • To prevent abuse (rate limiting, spam prevention, ban enforcement).
  • To display community content (posts, comments, profile pictures).

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • When required by law or to respond to legal process.
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or the public.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Cloudflare R2 — Used to store uploaded images (posts and profile pictures).
  • YouTube — Embedded videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com).
  • Neople (Dungeon Fighter Online) API — Used to fetch character data. Your DFO character information is publicly available through this API.

5. Data Retention

  • Account data is retained as long as your account is active.
  • You may delete your account at any time through the Account settings page. This permanently removes your account data.
  • Posts and comments made by deleted accounts remain visible but are attributed to "[deleted]".
  • Notifications are automatically deleted after 30 days.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures including password hashing, HTTPS encryption, rate limiting, and input sanitization. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

This service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out through the community or our GitHub repository.
